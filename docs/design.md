# Design

Small, clean and calm, and made of glass: a tool that lives in the tray. The look is **Glass** ([D0028](decisions/D0028-glass-design.md); reference: the Glass artboard of https://claude.ai/artifact/A4USeBEumQ4aNP6LoND9HB). Its surfaces are frosted and translucent, its shapes round (buttons and chips are pills) and its springs bouncy. Behind it all sit soft out-of-focus colour fields and a fine film grain. Everything is in `src/web/base.css`: tokens, type and components. A page's own stylesheet is layout only, and takes every colour, length, font size, border width, radius, shadow and filter from a token. `test/web/design.test.ts` fails on a raw value outside `base.css`, and checks the contrasts below. The structure (tokens, hairlines) is from [D0007](decisions/D0007-design-system.md).

Light and dark each have their own values. The glass tokens are translucent, so `design.test.ts` checks every contrast on the colour a reader sees: `test/web/glass.ts` composites a token over each ground it can sit on (the backdrop, alone and under each field, at the darkest and lightest the grain makes it; the glass over those; the floating glass over a black, grey and white desktop) and takes the worst. `bun test/web/glass-report.ts` prints the table, for tuning.

## Glass
- **The backdrop.** `<body class="backdrop">` (the chat view, the settings page) draws the window's ground (`--bg`), then soft colour fields (`--field-*`, large radial shapes blurred by `--field-blur`), then the grain over them. The grain is `--grain`: an inline SVG `feTurbulence` fractal noise at base frequency 0.85, two octaves, desaturated, tiled, and blended as an overlay at `--grain-strength`. It is lighter in light, where an overlay shows more. Every page uses this one class. None draws its own fields or grain.
- **Glass.** `.glass` is a frosted panel over the backdrop: `--glass` with `backdrop-filter: var(--frost)` and the `--glass-edge`. Its blur smooths the grain away under its text. `.glass-float` is the same over the real desktop, denser, with `--shadow`. The chat view's sidebar and stream, and the settings page's cards, are `.glass`. The pop-up, the snip's box, menus and the delete dialog are `.glass-float`.
- **Clear pages.** The dock and the pop-up float over the desktop. Their own page is transparent: the shell makes the windows see-through and blurs the desktop behind them (the OS's frost). The dock draws the glass's tint and rim and no backdrop-filter (below).

## Themes
The setting `theme` (Settings, Appearance) is `system`, `light` or `dark` ([D0018](decisions/D0018-themes-and-settings-page.md)). `src/web/theme.js` sets it on the page: `data-theme="light"` or `"dark"` on `<html>`, and no attribute for `system`, which leaves it to `prefers-color-scheme`. A page that follows the setting loads it first, as a plain script before its stylesheets: `<script src="/assets/theme.js"></script>`. It applies the value cached in `localStorage` at once, so a page never flashes the other theme, then the runner's (`GET /api/settings`), then each `settings` message on `/ws`, live. Every page loads it: the pop-up, the snip, the settings page and the chat view follow it, and the dock loads it too but stays dark (below). The chat view's sidebar has a gear, a square quiet button, that opens the settings page.

base.css has four sets of the colour tokens: `:root` (light), `prefers-color-scheme: dark`, `:root[data-theme="light"]` and `:root[data-theme="dark"]`. The forced sets are the schemes' own values, so one palette is designed per scheme and its contrasts (below, in `design.test.ts`) hold for both ways of choosing it; `test/web/theme.test.ts` fails when they differ and checks the contrasts again. An attribute on `:root` outranks the media rule's plain `:root`, so the order in the file doesn't matter. Change a colour in all of its sets together.

`.dark` and `.light` give everything inside them one scheme whatever the theme: the settings page's previews, and the snip's dim and labels. The dock's `<body>` has `class="dark"`: on `<body>`, not `<html>`, because theme.js puts `data-theme` on `<html>` and `:root[data-theme="light"]` outranks `.dark` on the same element. `.dark` repeats the dark scheme's values, and `test/web/dock.test.ts` fails when the two differ.

## Type
Geist for the interface, Geist Mono for code and the small labels (`.label`, `kbd`, the snip's size) (`--font`, `--mono`). Both are variable, latin and latin-ext, served from `/assets/fonts` with their OFL licences (from the `@fontsource-variable` packages, D0028). The pages ask no other host for anything. Weights are `--regular` 400, `--medium` 500 and `--semibold` 600, never synthesised. An `<em>` in a message is slanted by the browser (D0013).

| Size | For |
|---|---|
| `--text-xs` 11px | `.label` captions (uppercase, `--track-caps`) and key hints |
| `--text-s` 12px | `.small` meta lines, and all code (`.mono`, `code`, `pre`) |
| `--text-m` 13px | The interface and prose, line height `--leading` 1.5 |
| `--text-l` 16px | `.title` and the pop-up's task box, with `--track-l` tightening |

Times, durations and costs use tabular numbers (`time`, `.num`).

## Hairlines
Every border is `var(--hairline) solid` a line colour: one device pixel, so 1px, and 0.5px where a CSS pixel is two or more device pixels. Inside a panel, things are separated by hairlines and space. Panels are separated by their glass edge and the backdrop between them. A colour on a hairline carries meaning: amber around an open approval (with a soft amber glow), red around an error.

## Components
- **Button**: `<button>` or `.button` (a link), always a pill. Plain has a hairline; `.primary` is filled with the accent, one per view at most; `.quiet` has no border until hovered. `.small` is 20px tall. Under the pointer it lifts (`--lift`), pressed it squashes (`--squash`), on the bouncy curve over `--dur-press`.
- **Input**: `.input` on an `<input>` or `<textarea>`, on `--surface` with `--radius-m`. Focus is an accent border and a soft halo.
- **Dropdown**: `.select` on a `<select>` whose first child is `<button><selectedcontent></selectedcontent></button>` (so a long choice ends in "…"); `.quiet` and `.small` as for buttons. Code that rebuilds the options keeps that button. Closed, it is an input with a chevron, focused like one (accent border and halo; `.quiet` takes the plain ring). Open, its list floats like a menu (surface, `--shadow`, `--radius`, springing in on `--ease-snappy` and fading out): rows highlighted with `--selected` under the pointer or the keys, a muted check at the end of the chosen one. Drawn by `appearance: base-select`, so it stays the browser's select, keys and all; a browser without it shows the OS's list ([D0027](decisions/D0027-custom-select.md)).
- **Card**: `.card` with `--radius`. With `.glass` it is a frosted panel (the settings page's). Alone it is a lighter `--surface` inside the glass edge (the clipboard attachment). Padding is the page's.
- **Message bubble**: `.msg.user.card`, the person's own message: `--bubble`, its bottom right corner small.
- **List row**: `.row`, 28px or taller, hover and `aria-current="true"` (or `aria-selected`) backgrounds, no border.
- **Session list** (the chat view's sidebar, [D0033](decisions/D0033-sidebar-workspace-groups.md)): grouped by workspace. A group's heading is a `.group-head` button, a `.row` in `--text-s` medium `--muted` (the `--text` under the pointer): the workspace's name, its count in `--muted` regular, a chevron of two `--muted` hairlines at the end that points down when open and right when folded. Groups are a `--hairline` of `--line` apart; the groups with the newest session come first. Each session is one 28px `.row`: dot, its agent's glyph, the `>_` terminal mark if it came from a terminal, the title (ellipsized), "Pinned" in the accent, the time; the "…" button takes the time's place under the pointer. No row names its workspace. With one workspace, or one chosen in the filter, no heading is drawn. A heading (click, Enter or Space; `aria-expanded`) folds its group, remembered in `localStorage`; a folded group still shows the open session's row; while a search is typed every group with a match is open.
- **Agent mark**: `.agent-mark`, the glyph of the agent a session runs on (`agent.js`): Claude Code an eight-ray spark, Codex a hexagon, line drawings 1em square in the text's colour, `--muted` (the open row's in `--text`). Its name is the tooltip and the accessible name, never a word on a row. The session's head names the agent in words after the glyph. The pop-up's and the snip's agent switch is a quiet small pill with a `--line` hairline, the glyph and the name; turned, the glyph spins in on the bouncy spring ([D0034](decisions/D0034-agent-choice-in-the-pages.md)).
- **Status dot**: `.dot` with `data-status` set to the session's status. A lit dot glows (`--glow-*`). `running` breathes: it draws in to 0.65 and dims, then back.
- **Key hint**: `<kbd>`, one per key, its meaning in plain text after it. Every key a page names comes from `shortcuts.js` ([D0046](decisions/D0046-chat-hotkey-and-shortcuts-guide.md)), in the platform's words (⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃ on a Mac, Ctrl Alt Shift elsewhere); the global ones as the person recorded them.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `.sheet` with `.guide` (`shortcuts-sheet.js`): a calm panel with a head ("Keyboard shortcuts", Close) over a scrolling list, a `.label` per section, each row its meaning on the left and its keycaps (`.combo`, "or" between) on the right, rows a hairline apart. In the chat it floats near the top in `--glass-float` with `--frost`, two columns where there is room; in the dock it is the panel itself, in the island's colour. ? opens it away from a text box, Esc closes it and nothing else.
- **Tooltip**: `.tip`, the page's own (not the OS's): `--ink` on `--ink-text`, `--text-s`, `--radius-xs`, after half a second's rest of the pointer or at once on a keyboard focus, under the control (over it when there is no room) and kept inside its container (the island's panel in the dock). It fades in on `--dur-fade`; nothing under reduced motion. Its words: what the control does, then its key in brackets, "Switch session (⌘K)". Every glyph-only control has one, and every control an accessible name.
- **Well**: `.well`, a sunken block for code and output.
- **Tool call** ([D0051](decisions/D0051-quiet-tool-calls-and-working-mark.md)): `<details class="tool">` with `.tool-name`, `.tool-action`, `.tool-line`, `.tool-mark` in its summary and a `.tool-body`: one quiet 28px row until opened, with no card. The summary is `--text-s` `--muted` (`--text` under the pointer and open): the tool's source in medium (an MCP tool's server without "claude.ai ", e.g. "Gamma"), its action ("list deals"), then the line (`toolSummary`: a command, a path, or the input's telling text, never raw JSON when it has text; mono only for Bash). Opened, it gets `--surface`. Calls in a row fold into a **run**, `<details class="steps">`: one row of its own, `.steps-count` ("11 steps") and `.steps-line` (its first three sources, "+n" for the rest; the call under way, while one is), with the calls in `.steps-body` behind a hairline. Text, an approval or a new turn ends a run.
- **Working mark**: `.working`, the Ledge in `--muted` at the stream's end while the selected session's turn runs (`chat.html`'s `#working-template`). Its dot hops on the ledge (`hop`, `--dur-hop`): a squash, a rise of 0.7 `--rise` and a squash on landing, then a rest. Under reduced motion it breathes (an opacity fade) instead.
- **Approval card**: `.approval` with `data-state` `open`, `allow`, `deny` or `unanswered`; `.approval-head`, `-tool`, `-state`, `-path`, `-input`, `-reason`, `-actions`. Open, it is the one amber thing on the page; decided, it is as quiet as a tool call.
- **Setting**: `.setting` in a `.card`, the settings page's row: a name over one muted line on the left, its control on the right, rows a hairline apart. A choice of two is a `.segmented` radio group; on or off is a `.switch` (a checkbox, `role="switch"`); a shortcut is its keycaps on a quiet button that records the next keys pressed. A 400's error is a `.field-error` line in `--danger` right under the field it names, and the field's border turns red; "Saved" is a muted word at the top that fades.
- **Markdown**: `.md` on an assistant's message, drawn by `markdown.js` ([D0013](decisions/D0013-markdown-renderer.md)). Compact, a chat and not a document: blocks `--space-2` apart, headings at `--text-m` semibold (`h1` at `--text-l`, `h5`/`h6` muted), lists indented by one and a half times the type size with muted markers, task marks as small boxes (done: filled with the accent), quotes behind a `--line-strong` hairline in `--muted`, rules and table rows as hairlines, code blocks as `.well` that scroll sideways with their language as a quiet caption. Links are the accent, underlined in `--accent-soft`. `<em>` is the browser's slanted Geist, the one synthesis allowed. The person's own messages stay plain text.

## Motion
Things move like objects with a little weight, not on straight lines: they spring, and Glass is lively. Four springs, as CSS `linear()` curves, each with the duration it takes to come to rest:

| Spring | Feel | Duration | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--ease-gentle` | Near critical, under 1% overshoot | `--dur-gentle` 290ms | Something appearing or sliding without fuss: a page's contents as its window opens |
| `--ease-bouncy` | About 12% past its end, then back (the reference's `--spring`) | `--dur-bouncy` 680ms | Opening and arriving: the island, a peek, rows, the snip's prompt box, a clipboard attachment, an approval card, a menu, the workspace mark sliding |
| `--ease-soft` | About 1% (the reference's `--spring-out`) | `--dur-soft` 460ms | Closing: the island gathering back into its pill |
| `--ease-snappy` | Critical in effect, under 0.2% | `--dur-snappy` 190ms | A quick answer to a key or a click |

Always pair a curve with its own duration (`animation: x var(--dur-bouncy) var(--ease-bouncy)`): the curve is the spring's path over exactly that time. The one exception is a button's lift and squash, the bouncy curve over `--dur-press`, as the reference has it. Opacity doesn't spring: a thing that springs in also fades in on `--dur-fade` and `--ease`, as a second animation, and that fade clears a `--blur-in` as it goes (`filter`, in the fade's keyframes only), so content comes into focus. Leaving is a fade on `--dur-out`, quick and soft, with no spring.

Animate `transform` and `opacity` only (and the fade's clearing blur), never size or position: a change of size is a FLIP (set the new size at once, draw it where it was with a transform, let the transform go), as the pop-up's workspace mark does. Travel is small: `--rise` and `--pop`. Something that follows the pointer (the snip's selection while dragging) follows it exactly, with no easing; it may settle with a spring when let go. The focus never waits for an animation: put it in place first, then start the motion.

The curves come from `src/web/motion.js`. It turns a stiffness and a damping into `linear()` points and a settle time (gentle, snappy). It also holds the two springs copied point for point from the Glass reference (`COPIED`: bouncy, soft), each with the nearest spring of its model, which `motion.test.ts` holds it to within 0.006. `bun src/web/motion.js` prints the tokens to paste into `base.css`, and `test/web/motion.test.ts` fails when the two differ. A new spring is a new entry there, not a hand-written curve.

**Reduced motion.** Under `prefers-reduced-motion` base.css stops every animation and transition. It sets `--rise` and `--lift` to 0, `--pop` and `--squash` to 1, `--blur-in` to none, the stagger and the lag to 0, every spring to `linear` and every spring's duration (and `--dur-press`) to `--dur-fade`. A page that wants its fades back says so in its own `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` rule, naming only fade keyframes with `!important` (as `snip.css`, `prompt.css` and `dock.css` do): a short plain fade, nothing that moves.

**The chat view.** Something that happens while the page watches rises in on `--ease-gentle` (`.arrive`, set by `chat.js` on live events only). A session's history, loaded when it is selected, is drawn at once. The complete text that replaces streamed text doesn't rise again. The view is two glass panels over the backdrop: the sessions and the selected session. An approval card arrives with a bounce (`--ease-bouncy`, from twice `--rise` and `--pop`). A session's menu scales in on the bouncy spring. A tool row's body drops in on the bouncy spring while the rows below it glide down (a FLIP with the same spring, through the Web Animations API with the token's curve). Closing, the body fades out on `--dur-out`, then the rows below gather up on the gentle spring. Selecting another session cross-fades: a copy of the old stream, over the new one, fades out on `--dur-out` while the new stream and its head fade in. Folding a sidebar group: the rows go at once and the groups below gather up on the gentle spring (a FLIP); unfolding, the rows drop in from under the heading on the gentle spring with a fade while the groups below glide down, and the chevron turns on the same spring. Under reduced motion nothing glides: only those fades remain, and a group folds and unfolds at once.

## The mark
The logo is the Ledge ([D0044](decisions/D0044-the-ledge-logo.md)): a dot waiting on a ledge, off to one side. It is black and white only, black on light and white on dark, and never green or any status colour: the status dots are the app's, not the mark's. In the macOS menu bar it is a template image, 18 points at 1x and 2x, which macOS draws in the bar's colour; `markBitmap()` in `shell/src/geometry.ts` draws it per pixel, with the bar's edges on whole pixels. The app icon (window icons, notifications, and the tray off a Mac, where nothing tints it) is the mark in white on a black rounded tile (`tileBitmap()`; `src/web/mark.svg`, which is also every page's favicon). Beside the name, in the chat's sidebar and on the landing page, it is an inline SVG in `currentColor`, so it takes the text's token. It is also the chat's working mark (above, "Components"), in `--muted`: the one place the mark moves, and only its dot.

## The snip overlay
One window over each display, on the frozen screen. The rest of the screen is dimmed by the dark ground at `--snip-dim` (about half), with the grain in the dim at `--grain-dim`, fading in fast (`--dur-fade`) once the capture is up. The selection is cut out of the dim, so it shows the screen as it is, with no grain. Nothing in it is the accent: its edge is a neutral light hairline (`--snip-line`), a ring one device pixel wide (`--device-px`, which `snip.js` sets from the display's scale) just outside the selection, drawn on the device pixels the crop takes, as part of `--snip-edge`, with a soft light glow around it and a faint light inside the edge that lifts the area a little above the dim. Its corners have short marks two device pixels wide (`--snip-mark`, `--snip-mark-w`), left out on a selection too small for them. Its size, in device pixels, sits in Geist Mono on a pill of light glass (`--snip-label`) in the same light. The hint is a floating glass pill. The prompt box is `.glass-float`: the frozen screen is frosted behind it. Its workspace is an inverse pill (`--ink`).

The prompt box is centred on the selection, below it, above it when there is no room below, inside it at its bottom when neither fits, and kept on the display (`anchorBox` in `snip-geometry.js`). While dragging, the selection follows the pointer exactly. On release the corner marks settle in from a few pixels out on `--ease-bouncy`, and the box springs in on it (from `--pop` and `--rise` below its place) with the focus already in its task box. Esc, or a started session, fades the dim, the edge and the box out (`--dur-out`) to the frozen screen, then closes the window.

## The dock
One dark glass drop hanging from the top edge of the screen, the same in both schemes (`.dark`). It is drawn in `--glass-float`, dense enough to read as one object even over a white desktop. Its top corners are square, since it hangs from the edge. The bottom ones are as round as half its height allows, up to `--radius-l`: a pill when idle or peeking, a drop when open. They follow the spring with the shape. Its edge is the glass's rim (`--glass-rim`) on the three free sides. It has no drop shadow (its window is its size, so a shadow would be cut off) and no backdrop-filter: the frost is the OS's blur behind the see-through window, and a backdrop-filter under a clip that springs each frame would cost frames.

**Out of a Mac's notch** ([D0035](decisions/D0035-dock-from-the-notch.md)), the island is opaque `--notch` black instead of glass, with no rim and no `--island-quiet` dimming, so the notch and the island read as one shape. With nothing going on it is the notch itself, so nothing shows. With something going on a 76px wing each side, at the notch's height, holds the dot (left) and the words (right), never under the notch. The peek's line and the panel start below `--notch-h`. On a Mac display without a notch the shell hands the page a stand-in of the same shape ([D0037](decisions/D0037-stand-in-notch.md)); there the quiet island is a visible black tab in the menu bar's middle.

It is subtle before anything else. Idle is a sliver at most 22px tall and as wide as its words: a dot alone, 16px tall and at `--island-quiet`, when nothing is going on; muted words when something runs; colour (`--warn`, `--danger`) only for "needs you" and "failed". A peek is one 40px line. Open is as tall as its content, at most 480px.

The island's element is as large as the shell's window and never changes size ([protocol](protocol.md), "Dock"; [D0010](decisions/D0010-dock-fixed-window.md)). What is seen of it is its `clip-path`, whose size `dock.js` sets from the contract's numbers and the content; the edge is the island's colour showing around a surface clipped one hairline smaller. A change of state animates that clip, and the opacity, transform and clearing blur of the content, and nothing else: each state's content keeps its full size and place and comes in once the shape is most of the way there, so nothing reflows on the way.

The island moves like a droplet ([D0017](decisions/D0017-lively-dock-and-chat-motion.md)). Its drawn width and height are two registered lengths (`--island-w`, `--island-h`, `@property`) that only the clip reads, each on its own transition. Growing, the height leads on `--ease-bouncy` and the width follows `--lag` (40ms) later: a stretch down first, then out, a little past its size and back, like a drop. The clip's top inset is always 0, so the top edge stays on the screen's edge through the whole spring. The overshoot stays inside the shell's fixed window: a peek overshoots in both ways, but the open dock is as wide as the window, so it overshoots only in height, and only when its panel is shorter than the window. Closing gathers back into the pill on `--ease-soft`, the width a moment ahead of the height. The open panel and a peek's line scale in from `--pop` on the bouncy spring while their blur clears, and a peek's words slide in after it, one after the other. The pill's running dot breathes and glows. A new "needs you" nudges the dot once. Opened, the session rows and the selected session's newest events arrive on the bouncy spring, `--stagger` (40ms) apart. One that arrives on its own later rises in at once. **The chat is the notch, larger** ([D0042](decisions/D0042-chat-in-the-notch.md)). One click on the notch (the pill, a peek, the notch's band over the open dock or the head's empty part, or the head's expand button, two arrows drawn outwards at its end) grows the same island into the chat view: the same black (or dark glass) shape, from the same top edge, as large as the shell worked out for the display (about 910x726 on a 13-inch MacBook Air, 1180x820 on a 1920x1080 screen). It grows on `--ease-soft`, the height leading and the width `--lag` twice later, so it drops down first and then widens; a panel that size wobbling on the bouncy spring read as a toy. Stepping back down is the reverse on the same spring, in first and then up, and only once the shape is inside the open dock's rectangle does the open dock's cap hold it again. The chat's content is the chat view itself (`/?embed=dock`) in a frame that is loaded with the dock and kept laid out at its full size, out of sight (`visibility: hidden`, `inert`) while off; it comes in as the panel does, from `--pop` at the top a moment behind the shape, with a fade and no blur (a blur over a surface that large costs frames). Inside the dock the chat view drops its backdrop and its glass: the sessions and the session are two columns of the island's own black, a hairline between them, the head's and the reply's rules gone, the reply box as quiet as the dock's. Its sidebar head has a back-to-the-dock button (two arrows drawn inwards) beside the gear. Esc steps down one state at a time: chat, open, idle.

The open panel is kept calm: few controls in view, nothing that scrolls sideways ([D0032](decisions/D0032-calmer-dock-panel.md)), and the session read is most of it ([D0038](decisions/D0038-dock-session-switch.md)). From the top:
- **The head**, one line: the session shown as its own switch (its status dot, its agent's glyph with the agent's name as the tooltip, its title in one line that ends in "…", and a chevron), Stop while a turn runs, a quiet "…" for the rest (Open in chat, Continue in terminal, Rename, Pin, Archive, Keyboard shortcuts), New task, and at the very right the expand button that grows the island into the chat (Open in chat in the "…" does the same, D0042). What was done or refused ("Copied", "Couldn't stop: …") shows in the head for a moment.
- **The list**, closed until the switch is pressed, then under the head, pushing the stream down: the workspace switch (a `.select quiet small` with "All workspaces" and each workspace that has a session; the choice is the dock's own, kept in its `localStorage`, and the pill and the peeks still count every workspace), then at most about four rows, then it scrolls. A row is the status dot, the agent's glyph, the title in one line that ends in "…", the workspace (only under "All workspaces") and the time. Right-click gives Rename, Pin and Archive. Picking a row shows that session and closes the list; Esc closes the list, and a second Esc the dock. Since D0040 the list is a switch: a find field at its top (with the keyboard in it when open) beside the workspace switch, the rows in the dock's order (waiting, running, then the rest), the highlighted row `--hover`, and ⌘1..⌘9 in place of the time while ⌘ is held. Beside the head's switch, each other session at work is a quiet chip: its status dot and the start of its title, at most two, then "+n". Keys: ⌘K, ⌘1..9, Ctrl+Tab and Alt+arrows (Ctrl for ⌘ off a Mac); no page takes Option or Alt with a digit.
- **The stream**: long words, URLs and code blocks wrap. The list and the stream are `overflow-x: hidden`, and every scrollbar is base.css's thin quiet one.
- **The reply box**: what it is for now ("Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line", or why it is off) is its placeholder. The line under it shows only when a reply went wrong. It is quieter than a page's input: a faint `--line` edge, no rule above it, and focused only its edge firms up to `--line-strong`, with no accent ring, since the dock puts the keyboard in it on every opening.

Focus is always visible: a 2px accent ring (`:focus-visible`), except the open dock's reply box, whose edge firms up instead ([D0038](decisions/D0038-dock-session-switch.md)). The windows are desktop windows used with a keyboard and a pointer, so controls are 28px tall, not touch sized; below 700px the chat view stacks the sessions above the stream.
