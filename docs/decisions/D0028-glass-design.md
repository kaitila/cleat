# D0028: The Glass design direction

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: active. Task: T-0049. Supersedes the look of [D0007](D0007-design-system.md) (Inter, JetBrains Mono, opaque surfaces) and the springs' timing of [D0017](D0017-lively-dock-and-chat-motion.md). Their structure stays: tokens only in `base.css`, hairlines, the island's clip.

## Context
On 2026-11-15 the owner looked at three style directions on a Design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/A4USeBEumQ4aNP6LoND9HB): A · Graphite, B · Glass and C · Ink. The reference for B is that artifact's `project/Glass.dc.html`. The owner chose **B · Glass**, with a little more grain than first shown, for the whole UI: dock, pop-up, snip, chat view and settings. Behaviour and checks stay as they are. Only the look and the motion change.

## Decision
- **Glass over Graphite and Ink.** The surfaces are frosted and translucent, the shapes rounder, and the motion bouncier. Behind them are soft colour fields and a film grain. Graphite (opaque, quiet) and Ink (flat, high contrast) were not chosen.
- **Geist and Geist Mono**, self-hosted like the fonts before them. The latin and latin-ext variable woff2 files and their OFL licences come from `@fontsource-variable/geist` and `@fontsource-variable/geist-mono` 5.3.0, unpacked with `npm pack` in a temp folder. No dependency was added. Inter and JetBrains Mono are removed. Mono also sets the small labels (`.label`, `kbd`). Geist's italic isn't served: an `<em>` is still slanted by the browser (D0013).
- **Translucent tokens, and contrasts checked as composited.** `--glass` (panels over the window's backdrop) and `--glass-float` (over the real desktop) are rgba, and so are `--surface`, `--sunken`, `--hover`, `--selected`, the lines and the bubble. `test/web/glass.ts` composites each token over every ground it can sit on before measuring contrast: the backdrop, alone and under each colour field at full strength, at the darkest and lightest the grain makes it; the glass over each of those; and the floating glass over a black, grey and white desktop. Text then holds 4.5:1 with the grain on and on any desktop.
- **The floating glass is denser than the reference.** The reference draws the island at 0.56 over a mid-blue desktop. The real desktop behind the dock or the pop-up can be white, so `--glass-float` is 0.86 (dark) and 0.88 (light). The chat and settings panels keep the reference's 0.56 and 0.62, because their backdrop is their own. For the same reason the accent and the status colours moved a little from the reference's (#7c9cff to #9bb4ff on dark, #4b63e6 to #3148c8 on light). Running and needs-you keep the reference's #6ee7b7 and #ffc46b on dark.
- **One backdrop and one grain.** `body.backdrop` in `base.css` draws the fields (radial gradients blurred by `--field-blur`) and the grain (`--grain`: an inline SVG `feTurbulence` fractal noise, base frequency 0.85, two octaves, desaturated, tiled) as an overlay at `--grain-strength` (0.34 dark, 0.2 light). Only the chat view and the settings page use it. The dock and the pop-up are clear pages that float over the desktop: the shell makes their windows see-through and adds the OS blur (another task). The snip's dim carries the grain at `--grain-dim` (0.28), as the reference has it under a dim. The selection, which shows the screen as it is, has no grain.
- **No backdrop-filter in the dock.** Behind the dock's see-through window there is nothing for the page to blur, and a backdrop-filter under a clip that springs every frame costs frames. The OS blur is the frost there. The dock has no drop shadow either: its window is the island's size, so a shadow would be cut off.
- **The springs are copied, not made.** The reference's `--spring` (about 12% over, 680ms) and `--spring-out` (about 1%, 460ms) become `--ease-bouncy`/`--dur-bouncy` and `--ease-soft`/`--dur-soft`, point for point. `motion.js` holds them as `COPIED`, each with the nearest spring of its own model (stiffness 259, damping 18.25; 399 and 33.75), which `motion.test.ts` holds them to within 0.006. Gentle and snappy are still made by `motion.js`.
- **Motion from the reference.** The island stretches down on the bouncy spring and widens `--lag` (40ms) later. It closes on the soft spring. Its bottom corners stay as round as half its height allows, up to `--radius-l`. Content scales in from `--pop` (0.94) while a `--blur-in` (6px) clears in its fade. Rows arrive `--stagger` (40ms) apart. Buttons lift on hover and squash on press over `--dur-press` (380ms, the bouncy curve quicker, as the reference does). Lit dots glow and the running one breathes. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` all of these are short plain fades.
- **The design lint and the tests follow the new rules.** Radii are a scale from 6px to the 999px pill. A page may use `--glass-edge` as a shadow and a `filter`/`backdrop-filter` token. Keyframes may clear a blur in a fade-in. The dock's frame budget (under 34ms) is unchanged.

## Consequences
- The pop-up and the dock look right only once the shell's windows are see-through. Until then their clear page shows the window's own background around the rounded glass.
- A page that needs a frosted panel uses `.glass` (over its own backdrop) or `.glass-float` (over the desktop), never its own rgba.
- A new colour token goes in all four sets in `base.css`, and `test/web/glass.ts` must know its ground if text sits on it.
